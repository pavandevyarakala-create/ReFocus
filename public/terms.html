2. Description of Service & Core Mechanics

Re:Focus is an Android digital wellbeing and habit-building utility designed to help users track, understand, and regulate their consumption of short-form video formats (Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts).

  • Reel Counting & Detection: Re:Focus utilizes an Android Accessibility Service to detect when supported video reels are actively playing and counts scroll transitions. Real-time counts are displayed in an on-screen floating HUD.
  • Daily Limits & Skull Days: Users configure a target daily reel viewing limit. Exceeding your configured limit triggers a "Skull Day", which immediately resets your Current Streak to zero.
  • Discipline Levels: Your discipline status (Seedling, Focused, Consistent, Ironclad, Master, Legend) is determined by your lifetime Longest Streak. Your discipline tier represents lifetime best consistency and never decreases upon encountering a Skull Day.
  • Strict Mode: An optional habit-enforcement feature. When enabled and your daily reel limit is exceeded, the app triggers an automatic back navigation (GLOBAL_ACTION_BACK) to safely interrupt your feed consumption.
  • Rewarded Ad Adjustments: To foster mindfulness and prevent impulsive changes, altering a locked daily limit or disabling Strict Mode requires watching an AdMob rewarded video ad.
