Account & Data Deletion Request

Application: Re:Focus | Developer: FOCRAMEX | Last Updated: October 2026

Google Play Compliance Notice: This official web portal allows registered users of Re:Focus to initiate account deletion, manage deletion grace periods, or submit manual deletion requests without needing the mobile app installed.

1. How to Delete Your Account (In-App Procedure)

If you currently have the Re:Focus Android app installed on your device, you can delete your account directly inside the app at any time:

  1. Open the Re:Focus app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account or Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Review the confirmation details and tap Confirm Delete.

What happens immediately: Your active session ends and local device databases (Room SQLite cache, DataStore preferences) are wiped immediately from your phone. Your cloud profile enters the 7-day safety grace period.

3. What Data Is Deleted Upon Account Deletion

When your deletion request is processed and the 7-day grace period concludes, our automated Cloudflare backend executes a complete server-authoritative purge across all systems:

Data Category Specific Records Removed Storage Location
Authentication Identity Firebase Auth UID, registered email address, Google Sign-In profile link Google Identity Toolkit
User Profile Document User display name, avatar URL, creation timestamp, account settings (/users/{uid}) Cloud Firestore
Reel Tracking History Daily watch metrics, short-form video tallies, timestamps (/users/{uid}/daily_data/*) Cloud Firestore
Cloud Backups All periodic cloud snapshots and restored checkpoints (/users/{uid}/backup/*) Cloud Firestore
Subscription Status App entitlement records, trial metadata (/users/{uid}/subscription/*) Cloud Firestore
Device-Local Data Room SQLite database, active session logs, DataStore preferences Android Local Storage (Device)

4. What Data May Be Retained & Why

In accordance with applicable statutory laws, tax frameworks, and fraud prevention regulations, the following limited records are retained:

  • Statutory Financial & Tax Records: If you purchased a Pro membership, billing invoices, payment IDs, and transaction tax records are maintained by our payment partner (Razorpay) to comply with Indian GST laws and accounting audits. These records contain zero browsing, reel-watching, or device metrics. Retention period: Up to statutory tax requirement (typically 7-8 financial years).
  • Anti-Abuse Fraud Prevention: Cryptographic hashes of completed Razorpay webhook event IDs (razorpay_events/{eventId}) are retained to prevent double-crediting and replay attacks. They contain no personal user browsing history.

5. The 7-Day Waiting Period Explained

To prevent malicious account deletion from hijacked devices or accidental misclicks:

  • Transition to PENDING_DELETION: Submitting a deletion request places your account into a 7-day grace period.
  • Cancellation & Restoration: During these 7 days, you can cancel the request at any time either by signing in to this web portal and clicking "Cancel Deletion & Restore Account", or by signing into the Re:Focus Android app.
  • Permanent Purge: When the 7 days elapse, any incoming synchronization or status check triggers an irreversible backend purge of your Firestore subcollections, backups, and Firebase Authentication user.
  • Autopay Mandates: Requesting deletion proactively cancels any upcoming auto-renewal cycles with Razorpay so you are not billed during or after the waiting period.
