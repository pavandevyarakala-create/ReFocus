Frequently Asked Questions

Re:Focus is an Android digital wellbeing tool built by FOCRAMEX. It tracks your viewing of Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, counts every video transition, and empowers you to establish and keep healthy digital habits.

You set a daily reel allowance (for example, 50 reels). The on-device Accessibility Service detects when a supported video feed is active and increments the counter with each vertical swipe. If you exceed your limit, the day becomes a "Skull Day," resetting your current streak.

Free mode provides basic on-device reel counting and today's session metrics with local storage. Signing in unlocks a 7-day free trial. Pro membership unlocks the Goal Calendar, multi-day Analytics, Strict Mode feed interruption, cloud synchronization across devices, and daily cloud backups.

No video inspection data or second-by-second session logs leave your device. Only daily aggregate counts (such as total reels and watch time for the date) are synced to your private Google Cloud Firestore document if you are signed in with an active trial or Pro membership.

Strict Mode automatically triggers standard Android back navigation (GLOBAL_ACTION_BACK) when you try to watch reels after your daily limit has been reached. It locks in until midnight server time to stop impulsive overrides.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions inside the Android app under Settings → Manage Subscription. You can request account deletion in app Settings → Delete Account or online at our Account & Data Deletion portal, which wipes local device data immediately and permanently purges cloud data after a 7-day recovery period.
