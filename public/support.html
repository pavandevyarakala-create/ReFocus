Support & Troubleshooting Guide

Re:Focus Help Center | Maintained by FOCRAMEX Support

Need Immediate Help? We are here to assist you. If you encounter any bugs, unexpected detection behavior, or billing issues, please reach out directly to our engineering desk at support.refocus@gmail.com.

1. Common Troubleshooting Steps

A. Reel Counter Bubble Not Appearing

If the floating reel counter does not show up when watching Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts:

  • Verify Overlay Permission: Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Special App Access → Display over other apps, and ensure Re:Focus is enabled.
  • Verify Accessibility Service: Go to Settings → Accessibility → Installed Services, find Re:Focus, and confirm that the service toggle is switched ON.
  • Aggressive Battery Optimization: Some phone manufacturers (such as Xiaomi/MIUI, Samsung/OneUI, Oppo, and Vivo) aggressively kill background services. Please exclude Re:Focus from Battery Optimization (Settings → Apps → Re:Focus → Battery → Unrestricted).

B. Reel Counter Not Incrementing on Swipe

Re:Focus detects swipes and transitions between short-form video items:

  • Ensure you are in the vertical full-screen short-form video player of Instagram (Reels tab) or YouTube (Shorts tab). Standard in-feed carousel posts or horizontal video feeds are not counted as short-form reels.
  • If Instagram or YouTube recently updated, toggle the Re:Focus Accessibility service OFF and back ON once to re-bind the accessibility window listener.

C. Strict Mode Back Navigation Not Working

Strict Mode uses standard Android GLOBAL_ACTION_BACK accessibility triggers:

  • Ensure Strict Mode is toggled ON in your Re:Focus dashboard.
  • Check that your daily limit has indeed been reached or exceeded for the day.
  • Ensure the Accessibility Service remains enabled in system settings.

D. Cloud Sync or Backup Fails

  • Active Subscription or Trial Required: Cloud synchronization and on-demand cloud backups require an active Trial or Pro membership. Free-tier accounts operate exclusively in local device-only mode.
  • 24-Hour Backup Restriction: To protect server integrity, cloud backups can only be created once per 24 hours per user. If you already backed up today, the app will show your next eligible backup time.
  • Internet Connection: Ensure your phone has a stable Wi-Fi or cellular internet connection.

2. In-App Subscription Management

How do I manage or cancel an active subscription?

Subscriptions are managed directly within the Android mobile application:

  1. Open Re:Focus on your Android phone and navigate to Settings.
  2. Tap Manage Subscription.
  3. Tap Cancel Autopay and confirm your choice.

Your subscription mandate will be cancelled immediately with zero further billing cycles. Your Pro features will remain unlocked until the current paid period concludes.
