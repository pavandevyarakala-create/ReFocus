Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Application: Re:Focus | Brand: FOCRAMEX

Privacy-by-Design Promise: Re:Focus counts short-form video reels and calculates view durations entirely in real time on your physical Android device. We never capture, record, or transmit your screen contents, personal chats, keystrokes, passwords, or browsing history.

1. Overview & Privacy Philosophy

Re:Focus, developed and operated under the brand FOCRAMEX, is built on a strict privacy-by-design architecture. We believe that reclaiming your attention and building digital discipline from algorithmic short-form video feeds (Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts) should empower you without compromising your personal privacy.

This Privacy Policy transparently details what information we collect, what data is processed purely on-device, what information is synchronized with our secure cloud backend, how permissions are utilized, and your complete rights to access, manage, and delete your data.

2. Information We Collect and Process

We adhere to strict data minimization. We collect and process only the minimum information required to deliver reel habit tracking, streak accountability, entitlement verification, and cloud continuity:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Google Account Profile: When you sign in with your Google account via Google Identity Services / Firebase Authentication, we receive your email address, display name, unique Google user ID (UID), and profile picture URL. Free/unauthenticated users can use basic local tracking without providing any account information.
  • User-Configured Discipline Preferences: Your configured daily reel limit (e.g., 50 reels), goal intent, and Strict Mode preferences configured within app Settings.
  • Customer Support Correspondence: When you reach out via our support channel (support.refocus@gmail.com), we receive your email address and any details you provide to troubleshoot issues.

B. Information Generated Through App Usage

  • Daily Aggregate Reel Statistics: Total reels scrolled per day, total watch time spent in short-form video sessions (in milliseconds), active day status, and date stamps.
  • Platform Usage Distribution: The originating platform of short-form feeds (Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts) to display usage breakdown.
  • Discipline & Habit Milestones: Current consecutive streak count, lifetime longest streak, Skull Day occurrences (days where your configured daily limit was exceeded), and discipline tier levels (Seedling, Focused, Consistent, Ironclad, Master, Legend).

C. Device Attestation & Security Signals

  • Security & Environment Signals: Android operating system version, app version code, and installation integrity signals.
  • Cryptographic Attestation: Device integrity tokens verified via Google Play Integrity and Firebase App Check to prevent unauthorized tampering and protect backend endpoints.

3. Local On-Device Processing vs. Cloud Synchronization

To ensure total transparency, the table below clearly outlines what data stays strictly sandboxed on your device versus what is transmitted to our cloud servers:

Data Category Storage Location Transmitted to External Servers?
Video Detection & Scroll Transitions Processed in real-time in device RAM NEVER. Zero video inspection data leaves your device.
Screen Content & Personal Messages Never read, inspected, or cached NEVER. Re:Focus cannot access messages, keystrokes, or contacts.
Detailed Session Event Logs Encrypted SQLite Database (Android Room) on device NO. Detailed second-by-second session records remain on-device.
Daily Aggregated Summaries On-Device Room DB & Google Cloud Firestore YES. Synced to your private user document (/users/{uid}/days/{date}) for authenticated accounts with active Trial or Premium status.
User Profile & Preferences Jetpack DataStore & Cloud Firestore YES. Account profile, daily limit setting, and tour status synced to /users/{uid}.
Subscription & Payment Status Server-Authoritative in Firestore & Edge Workers YES. Synced for access verification and recurring mandate management.
Cloud Data Backups Cloudflare Edge & Cloud Firestore YES. Initiated on-demand (restricted to 1 successful backup per 24 hours per user).

4. Android Permissions & Why They Are Required

Re:Focus requests specific Android system permissions strictly to provide core habit-tracking and wellbeing functionality:

  • Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE): Required strictly to detect when supported short-form video players (Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts) are actively playing and to count scroll transitions. All detection occurs entirely in real time on your device. When you enable Strict Mode and exceed your limit, the service invokes the standard system back action (GLOBAL_ACTION_BACK) to safely exit the feed. Re:Focus NEVER reads or captures keystrokes, passwords, private messages, camera feeds, contacts, or interactions outside target short-form feeds.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Required to render the lightweight floating reel counter HUD directly over supported video apps, giving you instant real-time awareness without needing to switch apps.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): On Android 13+, allows Re:Focus to deliver daily limit threshold warnings (80% and 100% boundary alerts) and habit streak updates. This permission is optional and can be toggled in system settings.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Required to authenticate your Google session, securely verify subscription and trial entitlements, fetch authoritative server time, sync daily aggregate summaries, and process cloud backups.

5. How Collected Information Is Used

We use collected data solely for the following operational and product purposes:

  • Calculating real-time reel counts, session durations, daily progress, and discipline tiers.
  • Displaying the live floating counter overlay and enforcing user-configured limits or Strict Mode exit actions.
  • Synchronizing daily aggregated summaries and preferences across sessions and app reinstalls.
  • Verifying trial and recurring subscription entitlement through cryptographically signed server leases.
  • Restricting cloud backups to a secure 24-hour rate limit to protect infrastructure integrity.
  • Protecting our services against fraud, unauthorized API calls, and abuse via Firebase App Check and rate limiters.

6. Third-Party Service Providers Actually Used

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks. We integrate only reputable infrastructure providers required to operate Re:Focus:

  • Google Identity & Firebase Authentication: Manages secure user sign-in and session tokens. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Cloud database hosting your private user profile, subscription status, and synced daily summaries under strict owner-only access rules (isOwner(userId)).
  • Google Play Integrity & Firebase App Check: Validates app authenticity and device integrity.
  • Cloudflare Workers: Serverless edge API infrastructure managing server-authoritative time, lease signing, subscription verification, and account deletion workflows. (Cloudflare Privacy Policy)
  • Razorpay Software Private Limited: Secure payment gateway facilitating ₹79/month recurring autopay subscriptions via UPI autopay mandates and cards in India. Re:Focus NEVER collects, processes, or stores your credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or net banking passwords. All payment transactions are handled directly on Razorpay's secure PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant servers. (Razorpay Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): Serves user-initiated rewarded video advertisements (e.g., when adjusting a locked daily limit or toggling Strict Mode). AdMob may process device identifiers including the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) and diagnostic data for ad delivery and fraud prevention, governed by Google's Privacy Policy. AdMob SDK has no access to your video viewing metrics or accessibility service data. (Google Ads Privacy)

Note: Re:Focus does NOT integrate Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or Crashlytics SDKs. We do not track cross-app browsing activity.

7. Subscriptions, Payments & Billing

Re:Focus offers an optional 7-day free trial upon account creation, followed by a recurring monthly subscription priced at ₹79/month:

  • Recurring Billing: Subscriptions are processed via Razorpay recurring autopay mandates (UPI Autopay / Card mandates). You are billed automatically at each monthly billing cycle unless cancelled.
  • Cancellation: You can cancel your recurring autopay subscription at any time directly within the app via Settings → Manage Subscription → Cancel Autopay. Upon cancellation, your Pro features remain fully accessible until the end of your prepaid billing period, with no subsequent renewals.
  • No Stored Financial Data: FOCRAMEX does not touch, handle, or store card credentials, CVVs, or UPI PINs.

8. Data Retention, Backup Restrictions & Account Deletion

You have complete control over your account data and lifecycle:

  • Retention Period: Active account data (synced daily summaries and profile records) is retained in Cloud Firestore for as long as your account remains active.
  • Cloud Data Backup Limits: To protect server resources and prevent automated scraping, authoritative cloud backups are strictly restricted to one successful backup per 24 hours per user, enforced server-side.
  • In-App Account Deletion: You can initiate account deletion at any time via Settings → Delete Account.
  • 7-Day Soft-Delete Grace Period: When requested, your account transitions to PENDING_DELETION. If you change your mind, signing back in within 7 days allows you to restore your account.
  • Permanent Data Purge: Once the 7-day grace period expires, all user records in Firebase Authentication and all associated Firestore documents (/users/{uid} and subcollections) are permanently and irrevocably deleted from our production servers.
  • Immediate Local Data Wipe: Logging out or initiating account deletion immediately purges all historical session records and preferences from your device's local database.

For detailed step-by-step instructions on deleting your account, visit our Account & Data Deletion Instructions page.

9. Technical Security Safeguards

We enforce multiple layers of technical protection:

  • Transport Layer Security: All API communication between the Android app, Cloudflare Workers, and Firebase uses TLS 1.3 / HTTPS with Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS).
  • Server-Enforced Access Control: Cloud Firestore security rules strictly mandate owner-only access (request.auth.uid == userId) and field whitelisting. Client apps cannot modify subscription entitlement records or another user's data.
  • Rate Limiting & App Check: Cloudflare edge endpoints enforce multi-tier rate limiting and require valid Firebase App Check tokens.
  • Server-Authoritative Clock: All habit resets, trial periods, and backup cooldowns use cryptographic server time to eliminate device clock tampering.

10. Children's Privacy

Re:Focus is not directed to individuals under 13 years of age (or under 16/18 where applicable by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you believe a minor has created an account without parental consent, please contact us at support.refocus@gmail.com and we will promptly purge the account and data.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) 2023, EU GDPR, or California CCPA/CPRA), you have rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access & Portability: View your synced data within the app or request an export of your information.
  • Right to Rectification: Update your profile or goal settings directly in the app.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Delete your account and all associated cloud data via in-app Settings or by emailing support.
  • Right to Revoke Permissions: Disable Accessibility, Overlay, or Notification permissions at any time through Android system settings.
